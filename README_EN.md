  ### 📌 What is "Distilling Humans"?

  Take someone's **chat logs + work docs + personality tags**, feed them to an AI, and get an AI Skill that speaks, judges, and collaborates the way they do.

  Not copy-paste. Real understanding — how they talk, how they think, what they care about.
